Return Policy

Open-box video proof is must

For any damage, wrong product or return claim, an uncut open-box video is required. The parcel opening, product and issue should be clearly visible.

  • Record before opening the parcel fully.
  • Video should be clear and unedited.
  • Issue should be visible in the video.
  • Claims without proof may not be accepted.
